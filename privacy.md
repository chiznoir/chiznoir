# Privacy Policy

This page covers the personal Google OAuth app used by the owner of this GitHub account.

- The app is used only by its owner, through rclone, to sync the owner's own files with the owner's own Google Drive.
- It requests the drive.file scope, which allows access only to files that rclone creates or opens.
- Data moves only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or collected by anyone else.
- No analytics, tracking, or advertising are used.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/chiznoir/chiznoir/issues
